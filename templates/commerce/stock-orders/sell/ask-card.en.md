# What to ask the shop for: Stock orders

Take this to the visit and tick each item with the owner. Ask for these and nothing more. An item marked optional is only for a shop that wants it.

## Every shop

- [ ] **(Optional) The shop's WhatsApp number, in the WhatsApp Business app on the owner's phone (the shop can also start with its page alone)**
  The shop keeps its number and its app; the helper works next to them. It must be the Business app, not plain WhatsApp. The owner opens the app at least every two weeks, or the connection can drop. Without it, customers book and pay on the shop's page, and the owner signs in with their fingerprint and gets alerts in their app.
- [ ] **The owner's own phone number, on WhatsApp**
  This is where the helper asks for approvals, and where the monthly report goes. The owner replies "approve" or "deny". Practise one together.
- [ ] **(Optional) With WhatsApp only: a Facebook login with admin access to the shop's Meta business portfolio**
  WhatsApp needs it. The owner logs in themselves, on their own phone: you never see a password or a code.
- [ ] **(Optional) Only if the shop uses its own Gupshup account (the pilot's route): the owner signs up at Gupshup, adds the Operator as an admin, and the shop gives you the app name and the API key**
  The shop keeps the account, and its own Gupshup wallet pays Meta's charges and Gupshup's fee. The API key goes into the panel's key box only; never write it on paper or send it in a chat. Our message templates must show as approved in the shop's Gupshup app before the first message.
- [ ] **The name the shop is known by in public (signboard, Google or Instagram page)**
  The name WhatsApp shows has to match what the shop calls itself in public.
- [ ] **Be ready for Meta to ask the shop to verify its business later**
  Which documents Meta will accept for a small shop is not confirmed yet. Do not tell the shop that no papers will be needed.

## For this template

- [ ] **Each supplier the shop orders from: the name and the supplier's WhatsApp number**
  Orders go only to these numbers, and only an answer from the supplier's own number counts as theirs. Read each number back to the owner.
- [ ] **What the shop orders: for each item, the supplier, the pack (box, piece, bag) and the last price**
  The same item from two suppliers, or by the box and by the piece, is two lines. A box is never worked out as ten pieces.
- [ ] **The most one order may be worth**
  In whole rupees. It is a hard stop: the owner's yes cannot raise it. It must not be below the dearest single item.
- [ ] **The most a month's supplier orders may add up to**
  In whole rupees, and not below the most for one order. An order's amount is kept from this the moment the owner approves it.

> Do not promise how long setup takes, or that nothing else will be asked for. Meta decides what it needs, and it can change.
