Your stock-order helper on WhatsApp

An AI helper that does one job: it prepares your supplier orders and sends them only when you say yes.

One example

  1. 1

    The owner writes an order

    order 10 boxes of hair colour from Ravi Traders

  2. 2

    The helper shows the exact order as a card

    Order po-1 for your approval: 10 × hair colour (box), ₹450 per box, total ₹4,500, from Ravi Traders. Approve or decline.

    Nothing has gone to the supplier yet.

  3. 3

    You tap Approve, and only then does the order go to the supplier

    Now Ravi Traders gets the order. This month's budget keeps ₹4,500 for it.

  4. 4

    When the goods come, you write "received po-1"

    The helper marks it received. If the supplier goes quiet, it asks about the same order and never sends a second one.

Three things you can check

  • No order goes to a supplier without your yes, and it is always the exact order you saw.If anything changes after you saw the card, the old yes does not count and you are asked again.
  • When it is not sure, it asks you.If you write "10 hair colour" and it comes by the box and by the piece, it asks which one. It does not guess.
  • You mark what arrived.The supplier's OK means they will supply it, not that it has arrived. When it comes, you write "received po-1".
