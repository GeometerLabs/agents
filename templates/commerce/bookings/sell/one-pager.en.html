Your booking helper on WhatsApp

An AI helper that does one job: bookings and deposits, inside rules you set.

One example

  1. 1

    A customer writes

    Haircut tomorrow at 6pm, can you do it for ₹300?

  2. 2

    The helper answers

    Our haircut is ₹450, and I can't go lower than that. Wed 4 Nov, 6:00 pm is free. Pay a ₹200 deposit to confirm: [link]

  3. 3

    The customer pays, and it books

    The ₹200 goes to your own Razorpay account. The booking goes into your calendar, and the helper checks that it is there.

  4. 4

    At the end of the month you get the report

    • Bookings made
    • Deposits collected
    • Stopped by your limits
    • You were asked
    • Could not confirm
    • Not checked

Three things you can check

  • It can't go below your prices.Someone asks for ₹300 on a ₹450 haircut: it says no, and the report counts it.
  • It asks you before any refund over your limit.It also asks when a cancellation comes too late. You answer on WhatsApp: approve or deny.
  • Every month you see what it did, including mistakes.The line "Not checked" says what we did not look at.
