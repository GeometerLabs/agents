Your order helper on WhatsApp

An AI helper that does one job: pre-orders with an advance, inside rules you set.

One example

  1. 1

    A customer orders

    2 veg thali 10 nov 12:00 pm pickup

  2. 2

    The helper answers with the total and the advance

    2 × veg thali for Tue 10 Nov, 12:00 pm to 1:00 pm, pickup. Total ₹240. To confirm, pay ₹120 here: [link] (UPI ID annapurna@okicici, Annapurna Tiffins) (I'll hold your order for 30 minutes.) The owner confirms the payment, then I'll message you.

    The stock is held for 30 minutes.

  3. 3

    You see the money in your UPI app and tap Received

    Only then is the order confirmed, and you are told what to prepare. "I have paid" and a screenshot confirm nothing.

  4. 4

    On the 1st of the month you get the report

    • Advance marked paid by owner
    • To collect at pickup
    • Refunds marked sent by owner
    • Orders taken
    • Cancelled
    • You were asked
    • Stopped by your limits
    • Not checked

Three things you can check

  • No order is confirmed until you mark the advance received.The money reaches your own UPI ID and you see it in your own UPI app. A message or a screenshot confirms nothing.
  • It can't go below your prices or past your stock.A veg thali stays ₹120 whatever the customer says. It never takes more than the day's stock or the day's order limit, and no order after the cut-off.
  • A refund never goes out on the agent's word.You send every refund yourself. A cancellation too late for a refund, or a refund above your limit, comes to you to say yes or no.
