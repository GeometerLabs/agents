# What to ask the shop for: Only show what the shop has

Take this to the visit and tick each item with the owner. Ask for these and nothing more. An item marked optional is only for a shop that wants it.

## Every shop


## For this template

- [ ] **The name the shop is known by in public (signboard, Instagram page)**
  It is the heading of the shop's page, "What we have".
- [ ] **The town or village, and the pincode if the owner knows it**
  People nearby find the shop by it. The owner can change it later in their app.
- [ ] **(Optional) What the shop sells, in the owner's own few words**
  A label for you and the shop's page. Nothing is shown to anyone because of it.
- [ ] **The owner, with their own phone, at the visit; their phone number only if they have one and want it kept**
  You show a one-use sign-in code on the shop's page and the owner scans it with their own phone to make their passkey. No WhatsApp is needed.

> Do not promise how long setup takes, or that nothing else will be asked for. Meta decides what it needs, and it can change.
