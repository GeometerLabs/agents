# Set up "Only show what the shop has" (free)

For local Operators (18 and over). This is the short guide for the visit. The owner never reads it: they get the shop's page and their own app.

**What it is.** The shop shows what it has on its own page. The owner tells the assistant ("two Persian kittens, ₹8,000 each", with a photo), the assistant writes the listing, and the owner taps to show it. It takes no booking, order or fee, holds no money and sends no WhatsApp message. It is free.

**At the visit.** Bring [the card of what to ask for](ask-card.en.md). Set the shop up on the panel (name, town, what it sells, languages, the owner), then make the owner's first sign-in code on the shop's page and let the owner scan it with their own phone.

**What to say.** "Your shop's page shows what you have today. Nothing is shown on Streetlist or in search until you switch it on yourself."
