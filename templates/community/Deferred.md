# Deferred: what the planned community templates leave open

3 November 2026. All three are specs, so almost everything is open. This file lists what each one needs before it can stop being a spec in `templates/community/` and become a built template, and the questions that only a real group can answer. Nothing here is claimed as done anywhere else.

## Common to all three

| Left open | Why | Revisit when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The code.** No settings reader, no ledger, no reminders, no statements | Specs first | Fee and dues collection (B16) exists, then the shared modules are moved out of Bookings (B30) |
| **Records anyone can check (B28):** the signed statements, the verify code, the public daily fingerprint, the verify page on geometer.dev | Not built. The specs describe it as planned. The verify page needs the owner's yes before any deploy | B28 is built |
| **Voice (B29):** read-aloud statements, voice notes | Not built | B29 is built (Phase 2 in the plan) |
| **Legal review.** Chit funds, money lending, deposit schemes, trusts and societies, FCRA, DPDP | No lawyer has read any spec. The specs name laws to ask about, not conclusions | Before any launch, per state |
| **A payment provider that gives a group an account and a record the agent can read.** A self-help group or a committee may not be able to open one | If none will, UPI payments to the group's bank account become "marked by" entries too, and the statement says "not checked with the bank" for them | A provider is chosen and tried with a real group |
| **Price and who pays** | A group is not a shop. Open question for the founder | The founder decides; then the sell guides can name one |
| **The sell guides, in every language, and the one-page sheets** | The outlines are in each spec. A template is not ready to sell until its guide is | Each template is built |
| **The crash-test situations.** For each template: a cash payment marked by someone who is not allowed (refused), a reminder after a payment (never), a payment arriving twice, STOP, an approval by the requester (refused), "paid without a recorded approval" shown, a correction line instead of an erase, a message that shows an amount when the member has not allowed it, a statement opened without the member's code | Needs the code | The template is built; then add them to its hand check |
| **Native review** of all Hindi, Malayalam and Kannada wording | Machine-written until a native speaker reads it | Wording exists |
| **Quiet hours for reminders** | Bookings counts "hours before" and has no quiet time. These templates need "nothing at night" | Built with the first of them |
| **A real, measured deployment** (the rule for listing a template) | None. No group has used any of this | A real group is live and its record is measured |

## Self-help group

| Left open | Why | Revisit when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How a member with no smartphone gets her statement | The spec has a paper copy printed by the book-keeper; how it is checked at a meeting is not designed | A group is visited |
| Who sees disputes where the president and treasurer are relatives | One named person is chosen at setup; whether that is enough is unknown | A group is visited |
| Fit with state and national livelihood-mission books and bank-linkage records | The spec says it is a copy of the group's book, not a rival. Which existing digital books it must sit beside is not known | Someone who works with SHGs comments |
| The right second person to confirm a loan entry | Open question in the spec | A group is visited |
| Rotating savings and chit-like arrangements | The agent never runs one. Whether it may even record one a group runs on its own is a legal question | Legal review |

## Community fund

| Left open | Why | Revisit when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Whether committees want contributor names shown | The default is off. Festival lists are traditionally read out | A committee is visited |
| The shape of the approval rule | One approver or two of three, by amount. Many committees have one strong treasurer | A committee is visited |
| The balance check against the passbook | The treasurer types the balance and adds a photo. It shows a gap; it proves nothing. Whether committees will do it is unknown | A committee is visited |
| Gifts in kind (labour, food) with no bill | The spec asks how the statement should say it | A committee is visited |
| Temples run by the state or a board | A separate committee may not be allowed to collect | Legal review, per state |
| Foreign gifts (FCRA) | The spec says ask before accepting; no handling is designed | Legal review |

## Meeting follow-through

| Left open | Why | Revisit when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Where the speech-to-text and the drafting model run,** and whether a transcript may leave the builder's server | Cost, quality in Hindi, Malayalam and Kannada, and privacy are not settled. The engines are compared in the model eval with real voice samples (B29) | The voice module is built |
| Whether a voice note from one person is enough for most groups, and whether a full recording would ever be agreed | The spec makes the voice note the default | A group is visited |
| Evidence in a village with weak signal | A photo now and a location later is one option | A group is visited |
| What counts as "the counterpart's own record" for a panchayat or a block office | Open question in the spec | Someone who deals with them comments |
| The link to SabhaSaar (Gram Sabha minutes) | The spec says this must not be presented as an alternative, and to check its scope | Before launch |
| Task owners outside the group (a vendor, a technician): how they consent and leave | Designed only as "own task, with consent" | The template is built |
| Whether to keep a fingerprint of the transcript after deleting it | Kept out for privacy; it would let someone later ask what a decision was based on | Legal review and a group's view |
