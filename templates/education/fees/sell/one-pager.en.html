Your fee helper on WhatsApp

An AI helper that does one job: monthly fee reminders with a payment link, inside rules you set.

One example

  1. 1

    The helper writes to the parent

    Fee reminder from Bright Tuition Centre. Rohan Mehta's class 10 fee for Nov 2026 is ₹1,800, due on Thu 5 Nov. Pay here: [link] Reply STOP if you do not want these reminders.

  2. 2

    A parent asks for a discount

    Please take ₹300 off the fee

    Your limit is ₹100, so the helper asks you on WhatsApp. You reply "approve q-1", and the parent gets a new link for the new amount.

  3. 3

    The parent pays, and gets a receipt

    The money goes to your own Razorpay account. The helper reads the payment from Razorpay's record, then sends the receipt. Once the record shows paid, no more reminders.

  4. 4

    On the 1st of the month you get the report

    • Collected
    • Still to collect
    • Given back
    • Taken off fees
    • Reminders sent
    • You were asked
    • Stopped by your limits
    • Not checked

Three things you can check

  • A fee the payment record shows paid gets no reminder.Paid is judged from Razorpay's own record, never from what a parent says. If a parent says "I already paid" and nothing is on record, it tells you and marks nothing paid.
  • It can't take off more than your limit without asking you.Up to ₹100 for one child in one month it can do by itself. Above that it asks you. A refund a parent asks for goes to you, and one above ₹500 needs a one-use code.
  • Every month you see what it did, including mistakes.The line "Not checked" says what we did not look at.
